Bhadauriya AI

Your Personal AI, Powered by LLaMA 3.3 70B

I built Bhadauriya AI from the ground up. It can hold a conversation, understand images, and respond to voice input. Under the hood, it uses Groq's LLaMA 3.3 70B model, so replies come back quickly.
